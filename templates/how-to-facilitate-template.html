Before the Course Starts

Below are the steps you need to complete as the instructor of [[COURSE CODE]]. Pay particular attention to the [[PUBLISHER PLATFORM]] setup and assignment dates before students begin the course.

I. Review Course Assignment and Due Dates

Before you begin configuring [[PUBLISHER PLATFORM]] or Canvas assignments, you need a finalized due-date schedule for your specific course length. This schedule will drive every date you enter in the setup steps that follow, so it's worth confirming first.

Step 1: Confirm Your Start and End Dates

[[COURSE CODE]] is offered in multiple formats. Identify which one you are teaching (this can be determined by reviewing your course Start and End date located in Settings from the Course Navigation Menu) before mapping out dates.

Step 2: Assign Due Dates in Simple Syllabus

Open your Simple Syllabus from the Course Navigation Menu. Add the appropriate due dates in the table under Course Assignment Schedule. You can use this table as a reference for when you assign dates in [[PUBLISHER PLATFORM]] and to Canvas-native assignments.

Why this matters: Mismatched dates between [[PUBLISHER PLATFORM]], Canvas, and Simple Syllabus are one of the most common setup errors. Finalizing your schedule here first — before touching [[PUBLISHER PLATFORM]] — prevents having to redo the setup steps later.

III. Use Dropout Detective in Canvas

Dropout Detective is one of your most valuable tools for monitoring student engagement and identifying students who may need support. It brings together Canvas data such as missing assignments, zeros, grades, recent submissions, activity, and overall risk so you can quickly identify patterns and intervene early.

Use Dropout Detective throughout the term to:

  • Monitor First Week Attendance. Review student activity and participation to help identify students who may not have begun engaging with the course.
  • Prepare for 60% Participate reporting. Use student activity, submissions, missing work, and other course information to help evaluate participation before completing the required MySPC reporting.
  • Identify students who may need help. Watch for increasing risk scores, missing assignments, zeros, low grades, or declining activity.
  • Reach out early. Use the information in Dropout Detective to guide timely student communication and document outreach when appropriate.

Make Dropout Detective part of your regular course routine. A quick review can help you identify a student who needs support before missing work becomes a larger problem.

After the Course Ends

Before your course ends, take a few minutes to review grades, resolve any outstanding items, and submit final grades. Completing these steps carefully helps ensure that students' final grades are accurate and properly recorded.

Review, Finalize, and Submit Final Grades

Step 1: Review and Verify Final Grades in the Canvas Gradebook

Before submitting final grades, review the Canvas Gradebook and make sure all assignments, discussions, and other graded activities have been graded. Check for any missing scores, ungraded submissions, or other items that may affect a student's final grade. Review each student's final course grade for accuracy. No cell should be left blank with just dashes. Make sure all applicable grades have been entered and that the final grade shown in Canvas reflects the student's completed coursework before beginning the final grade submission process.

Step 2: Submit Final Grades

Follow the instructions for Submitting Final Grades in Canvas.

Need Help?

If you experience a technical issue while submitting final grades or have questions regarding an Incomplete, contact the Academic Technology and Support Team (ATST) using the Submit a Request form.
